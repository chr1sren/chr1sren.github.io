About

I'm an undergraduate at the University of Toronto studying Computer Science and Applied Mathematics. I am interested in learning-based control for robotics, and in particular in how robots can learn representations of the world that capture what actually matters for control. I hope to build methods that are both principled and practical.

Outside of research, I read mathematics textbooks for fun; math has a habit of offering a surprising idea or a deeper understanding when I least expect it. I recommend everyone read The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Mathematics. Lately I've been especially drawn to geometry and topology.

I enjoy traveling (who doesn't?). Outer Wilds is my favourite video game. I am proud of my hometown.
