Summary

Robots that learn from images need to ignore what does not matter, such as a moving background. Bisimulation methods group together situations with the same rewards and futures, but this can merge situations that call for different actions. We added an action-aware term that keeps them apart, and it outperformed the rest of the bisimulation family in simulation. But the family as a whole still fell behind simpler augmentation-based methods under strong visual changes, which showed us where bisimulation helps and where it does not.

The reading and thinking behind this project also shaped the question I hope to pursue in graduate school.
